# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

The FAQ section is designed to address the most common questions and concerns that users may have about DOCS. FAQs should provide clear, concise answers to recurring topics, helping users quickly resolve issues or understand key concepts without searching through the entire documentation.

## What Should FAQs Address?

- Common setup or installation problems
- Typical usage questions and best practices
- Clarifications about features, limitations, or terminology
- Troubleshooting steps for frequent errors
- Where to find additional help or resources

Well-crafted FAQs improve the user experience by reducing friction and making it easier for users to get started and succeed with DOCS.
